Your Cravelist

Every spot you want to try, in one list.

Stop digging through screenshots, saved posts and group chats. Add a place in seconds and it shows up on everyone's phone instantly.

  • Search any restaurant, café, bar or bakery, with spots near you first
  • Crave fills in the cuisine for you; add a note on why it made the list
  • Filter by cuisine, or by brunch, coffee, drinks, date night and more
Save from Maps

Spotted it? Save it.

Found somewhere on Apple Maps or Google Maps? Share it to Crave and it lands on your Cravelist in a couple of taps, without leaving Maps.

  1. Open the place in Apple Maps or Google Maps
  2. Tap Share, then Crave
  3. Pick a list, then Save
Rate it together

Score it as a crew.

After you visit, everyone rates it from 1 to 10, adds a review and shares photos. Crave shows the crew's average, and anywhere averaging 9.0 or higher earns a MUST.

  • See who's rated and who's still holding out
  • Everyone's photos and reviews in one place
  • Opening hours, directions and booking links built in
The Spin

Can't decide? Spin.

Crave picks a random spot from your Cravelist, or brings back an old favorite with Nostalgia mode. Narrow it down by cuisine or occasion first, then let fate decide. No take-backs.
