Questions

How do I invite someone to my Cravelist?

Go to Profile, find your list and tap the invite button next to it. You'll see a 6-character invite code you can share. Your friend taps Join a Cravelist in their Profile and enters the code.

Can I have more than one Cravelist?

Yes. Start as many as you like (one with your partner, one with friends, one for a trip) and switch between them from the list name at the top of the Cravelist.

How does the Crave score and MUST badge work?

Everyone on a list can rate a place from 1 to 10 after visiting. The Crave score is the average of everyone's ratings, and a place averaging 9.0 or higher gets a MUST badge.

Why does Crave ask for my location?

Only to show nearby places first when you search for a spot to add, and to show where you are on the Passport map. It's optional; search works without it. You can change it any time in Settings → Crave → Location.

Who can see my places, ratings and reviews?

Only the members of the Cravelist they're in. Crave has no public profiles or feed.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and profile. See the privacy policy for exactly what's removed.
